Features

Everything runs
inside your browser

No cloud APIs. No subscriptions to external services. Just your browser and a state-of-the-art local Whisper model.

Real-Time Transcription

Capture audio from any browser tab and transcribe it instantly with Whisper-grade ASR models running locally via WebGPU/WASM.

Instant Capture

Works with any tab audio — YouTube, Meet, Zoom web, podcasts, local files. No virtual cables or complex routing.

GDPR by Design

Your data never leaves your machine. No DPA to sign, no third-country transfer to worry about. Compliance without the paperwork.

Session History & Export

Automatically save every transcription. Search full-text across all sessions, organize with tags, and export as .txt, .srt, or .vtt.

Floating Overlay

Enable live captions directly over the video or page you're watching. No need to switch tabs or split your screen.

Smart Voice Detection

Built-in VAD ignores silence and background noise. Only transcribes when someone is actually speaking. No false positives.
